Data structures#

All are immutable: frozen dataclasses, and a named tuple for StructureRow. Coordinates are 1-based and inclusive, and seq_from is greater than seq_to for a hit on the reverse strand. Pairs are (i, j) tuples of 0-based positions with i < j. Structures are WUSS strings, with pseudoknots as letter pairs.

HitSelector

io.selection

A sequence, or one hit on it, to choose, optionally within one family

family, name, seq_from, seq_to

Hit, ReferenceHit and HitResult all have family, name, seq_from and seq_to, so choose works on each of them. HitTable holds Hits or ReferenceHits, and the protocol io.hits.HitLike names what it needs of them.

HitTable ranks hits by the lowest E-value, then the highest bit score, then the lowest family accession, wherever two families compete:
